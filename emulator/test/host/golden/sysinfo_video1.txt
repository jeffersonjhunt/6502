OO-1 SYSTEM INFORMATION

SYSTEM
  CPU        W65C02S at 7.159 MHz
             #,###,### Hz by the clock chip, no video frame to measure by
  ROM BIOS   0.14
  OODOS      0.9, its BIOS 0.2; OOLIB interface 9
  OOUI.BNK   none on A:
  MEMORY     1,024 KB fitted
             for programs: a 47K TPA, 320 KB in banks 1-5 (320 KB free)
  BOOTED     from the flash, at power-on

CARDS
  ADDR   ID   CARD                          REVISION
  $C300  --   serial: the stand-in 6551 (no ID register)
  $C380  $71  mass storage (SD)             1
  $C400  $81  DMA controller                1
  $C480  $91  video                         1 (not known to SYSINFO)
  $C500  $A2  sound                         2
  $C580  $B1  USB, game port and speaker    1
  $C600  $C1  Disk II controller            1
  $C680  $D1  north bridge                  1
  $C700  $E1  console (front panel)         1

VIDEO      revision 1, which OODOS no longer drives

SOUND      revision 2: two SIDs, the 6510 and its 64 KB
             an ES5503 and 128 KB of wave RAM
             raw PCM and MP3 (CODECS $83)
             free: 6510, stream, ES5503

STORAGE
  SD CARD    SDSC, 65,536 sectors (32 MB), FAT, firmware 0.2
             named "card"
             unit 1  A.IMG, 16,384 sectors
             unit 2  B.IMG, 16,384 sectors
  DRIVES     A: 8,192K, #K free
             B: 8,192K, #K free
             M: 128K, #K free
             O: and P:, the Disk II's (not looked at)

INPUT      USB keyboard, USB mouse
             game port: paddles 0 0 0 0, no button down
             joysticks 1 and 2: nothing pressed (or nothing plugged in)

CLOCK      2026-10-30 hh:mm:ss, the oscillator kept time; 25.00 C

PANEL      switches $00000, HALT/ENABLE at ENABLE, S INST
